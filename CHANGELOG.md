# EzGrimoire

## v1.0.0

- First version, for warlocks.
- `/grimoire` (or a key binding, or the addon drawer) shows a page per demon: every ability, every rank, and the level each grimoire needs. Green = your demon knows it, gold = the one to buy now, grey = skip it (a higher rank you can already use replaces it) or not yet. Hover a rank for the grimoire's price.
- Each demon's page remembers what it knew the last time it was out, so your Voidwalker's page stays right while the Imp is summoned. A demon you haven't summoned since installing it shows what it *may* still need until you summon it once.
- "Buy now" lists the grimoires worth buying at your level with their total price, and "Next at level N" shows what's coming.
- At the demon trainer the list opens beside the shop, and each grimoire is tagged Known, Learn, Skip, No demon or the level it needs. Grimoire tooltips (bags, auction house) say the same.
- When you level up, a chat line says which new grimoires just opened for your demons.
- Works in WoW Forever (with the Incubus) and Classic Era.
